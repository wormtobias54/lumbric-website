02 / About me

Hi, I'm
Tobias Worm.

Programmer from Belgium.

Lumbric Studios is my solo game development project. I've always wanted to make my own games, so I decided to give it a try.

I enjoy all kinds of games, from puzzles and indie titles to third-person action. I'm a big fan of Elden Ring, though I'm keeping my own projects smaller for now.

Why Lumbric?

Lumbric is a reference to worms, and my last name is Worm. That's where the studio name comes from.
